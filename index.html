Description

Lust Goddess is a turn-based gacha RPG where you recruit a squad of mercenaries and lead them through strategic battles. You collect girls across multiple factions, each with distinct roles like tank, DPS or support. Build synergies between your units, upgrade their skills and unlock subclasses to fine-tune your strategy.

Battles play out on a grid where you drag and drop units into position, trigger abilities and chain combos to take down enemies. Switch to auto-battle when you want to farm faster. Between fights, you interact with your squad through dialogue sequences and relationship progression to unlock exclusive rewards.

Beyond the campaign, you compete in PvP arenas, take on limited-time events and chase new character drops that rotate regularly.
